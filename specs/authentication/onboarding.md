# Onboarding

- There shall be a heading: "Pick the name the community will see"
- Below the heading there shall be a dimmed line:
  "This is your public identity on Wiki dance."
- There shall be a form with the following fields, in this order:
  - Artist name (text input) - Required, must be unique.
    This is the public name. An optional profile picture (file upload)
    shall sit next to this field.
  - A "Private" group. It must be specified that this information won't be public:
    - First Name (text input) - Required
    - Last Name (text input) - Required
  - Bio (textarea, not a rich-text editor) - Optional, max 300 characters
- There shall be a "Complete" action in the header and a "Complete" button
  at the bottom of the form.
- Once the user clicks "Complete", they shall be redirected to the homepage.
