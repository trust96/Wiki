# Notifications

- There shall be a notifications page in the reserved area.
- The page shall list the outcomes of the user's submitted edits.
- Each item shall show whether the edit was Approved or Declined,
  and the reason given by moderation.
- An Approved item shall offer a way to open the published wiki.
- A Declined item shall offer an "Edit content" action.
  When clicked, it shall open the section form for that content,
  with the decline reason visible.
